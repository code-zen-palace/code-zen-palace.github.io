Delete your Tiny Acres data

Tiny Acres has no accounts and keeps your game data on your device, so you are always in control. We never receive it.

In the app

  1. Open Tiny Acres → Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

This permanently erases your results, streak, in-progress games and settings on that device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, it also deletes the copy of your progress kept in your Play Games saved games (see Google Play Games below). Your Tiny Acres Plus purchase remains linked to your Google account and can be restored.

Without the app

Uninstalling Tiny Acres deletes its private storage, including all game data.

In your web browser

If you play today's acre in a browser at primekampllc.com/tiny-acres/play/, your results, streak and settings are kept only in that browser. Tap Delete my data at the foot of the game's page, or clear the site data for primekampllc.com in your browser's settings.

Android backup

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may also keep a copy of the game data in your own Google account backup (we cannot see it). To remove it, turn backup off or delete the backup in Android Settings → Google → Backup, or in your Google account's backup settings.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, Tiny Acres keeps a copy of your game progress in your Play Games saved games, so that it can come back on a new phone, and Google Play Games keeps your leaderboard scores. Tapping Delete my data in the app while you are signed in to Play Games deletes the saved copy as well. If Play Games cannot be reached at that moment, the app deletes it the next time it can, and never brings that progress back in the meantime. You can also delete your Play Games data, including this copy and your scores, at any time in your Play Games profile or your Google Account.

Advertising data

Advertising data held by Google AdMob is controlled through your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID / Reset advertising ID) and, in the EEA/UK/Switzerland, through "Ad privacy options" in the app's settings.

Anything else

Email hello@primekampllc.com from the address you want us to reply to. We hold no personal data about players ourselves, but we will help you with any request within 30 days.

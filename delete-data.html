Delete your Tiny Acres data

Tiny Acres has no accounts and keeps your game data only on your device, so you are always in control.

In the app

  1. Open Tiny Acres → Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

This permanently erases your results, streak, in-progress games and settings on that device. Your Tiny Acres Plus purchase remains linked to your Google account and can be restored.

Without the app

Uninstalling Tiny Acres deletes its private storage, including all game data.

Advertising data

Advertising data held by Google AdMob is controlled through your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID / Reset advertising ID) and, in the EEA/UK/Switzerland, through "Ad privacy options" in the app's settings.

Anything else

Email brandemak@gmail.com from the address you want us to reply to. We hold no personal data about players ourselves, but we will help you with any request within 30 days.

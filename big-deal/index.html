Big Deal

One large-print solitaire deal a day.

The same deal for everyone, checked by computer so it can always be finished. No timer, and unlimited undo.

Coming soon to Google Play

Its name on Google Play: Solitaire Daily: Big Deal

What it's like

  • Large print, in the card size you like: Normal, Large or Extra large.
  • A four-colour deck, high-contrast cards and a left-handed layout, if you want them.
  • Tap a card to move it. No clock, and undo as often as you like.
  • Finish under par for a gold medal, and keep your streak going.
  • Play past deals from the archive.
  • Plays fully offline.

Fair and simple

Free to play, with a few ads that never appear inside a deal. The first hint in each deal is free. Remove all ads is one optional, one-time purchase. No subscription, no in-game currency, no loot boxes.

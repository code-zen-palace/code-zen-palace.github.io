Travel

TravelCharika

When to go, and where to book.

A travel website and Android app. Each place's twelve months are scored from ten years of measured weather and the holidays that crowd it, so you can see the best time to go.

Website Android app in testing

Visit travelcharika.com Android app on Google Play (in testing)

What it's like

  • Every place's twelve months, scored from ten years of measured weather and the public holidays that crowd it.
  • Which months are good for hiking, the sea, skiing and the northern lights.
  • The big festivals, carnivals and markets, month by month.
  • Where to book, once you know when to go.

How it's paid for

TravelCharika is free to use. Some of the booking sites it lists pay it a commission; that never decides which sites are listed or in what order.

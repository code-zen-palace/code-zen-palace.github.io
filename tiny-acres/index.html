Tiny Acres

A cozy puzzle a day, in a painted 3D countryside.

One shared puzzle a day: match the edges, and your acre grows into one little landscape. And Worlds: whole landscapes to piece together. No lives, no timers.

The 3D look

Every tile, every world and the home screen are redrawn in a painted 3D look: woods, wheat fields, lakes, cottages and snow-capped peaks, with a living countryside behind your board. Close a forest and birds lift from the trees; close a lake and a fish leaps; close a village and its windows light up. Each day's acre now has a cozy name of its own, like Hazel Corner. And from Tuesday 24 November, every day's acre has all five terrains, and every day is just as friendly, so any day is a good day to start.

What it's like

  • Match forests with forests and lakes with lakes, and close regions for bonus points.
  • Painted 3D tiles: woods, wheat fields, lakes, cottages and snow-capped peaks, on a little island with the countryside behind it.
  • Beat the day's target for a medal, keep your streak, and share your finished acre. Friends can play the same acre and see both side by side.
  • Worlds: landscapes to piece together at your own pace, each in its own season. Every world is free.
  • Cozy acoustic music, festivals, tile colours and a dark mode. Plays fully offline.

Fair and simple

Free to play, with a few ads that never interrupt the daily acre. Tiny Acres Plus is one optional, one-time purchase that removes them. No subscription, no in-game currency, no loot boxes.

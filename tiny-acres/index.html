Tiny Acres

A cozy puzzle a day, and acres more.

One shared puzzle a day: match the edges, and your acre grows into one little landscape. Then Acres, numbered acres without end along a country lane, and Worlds: whole landscapes to piece together. No lives, no timers.

Acres

Acres takes the big card on your home screen: numbered acres without end, played one after another along a country lane. Finish one, tap Next acre, and a little cart rolls on to the next, with a new backdrop behind your board every acre and milestone flags along the way. New worlds too: Sandy Cove, a sunny bay with dunes, beach huts, a lighthouse and a jetty, and Petal Fields, with tulip and lavender strips, blossom groves and a glasshouse. Worlds now call their places by name, like Village square. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard for Acres and Worlds, while today's acre stays the same for everyone. And the look follows your day: bright by day, dark at night. Free play makes way for Acres; your bests stay in Stats.

What it's like

  • Match forests with forests and lakes with lakes, and close regions for bonus points.
  • Acres: numbered acres without end along a country lane, a little cart rolling on to the next, and a new backdrop behind your board every time.
  • Beat the target for a medal, keep your daily streak, and share your finished acre. Friends can play the same acre and see both side by side.
  • Worlds: landscapes to piece together at your own pace, from Meadow Valley in spring to Sandy Cove's sunny bay and the flower strips of Petal Fields. Every world is free.
  • Easy, Medium or Hard, painted 3D tiles, cozy acoustic music and festivals; bright by day, dark at night. Plays fully offline.

Fair and simple

Free to play, with a few ads that never interrupt a puzzle. Tiny Acres Plus is one optional, one-time purchase that removes them. No subscription, no in-game currency, no loot boxes.

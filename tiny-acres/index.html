Tiny Acres

A calm daily hex-tile puzzle.

The same stack of tiles for everyone; no lives, no timers, no way to lose.

On Google Play Play in your browser iPhone: coming

Get it on Google Play Play today's acre in your browser

What it's like

  • Match forests with forests and lakes with lakes, and seal off regions for bonus points.
  • Beat the day's target, keep your streak, and share your little map.
  • Cozy acoustic music, seasons and festivals, and a dark mode.
  • Plays fully offline.

Fair and simple

Free to play, with a few ads that never interrupt the daily acre. Tiny Acres Plus is one optional, one-time purchase that removes them. No subscription, no in-game currency, no loot boxes.

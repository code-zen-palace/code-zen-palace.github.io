Worlds

Piece together three landscapes, Meadow Valley, Lakeside and Snowy Mountains, one acre at a time. Each acre is cut into hexagon pieces with one right place: hold a piece over a gap and gold sides show where it fits. A free hint each level, and every area is free. Worlds is in the apps; the web page stays the daily puzzle.

What it's like

  • Match forests with forests and lakes with lakes, and close regions for bonus points.
  • Natural 3D tiles: wheat rows, woods, cobbled villages and lakes with shallows, and a finished acre grows its gaps into one landscape.
  • Beat the day's target, keep your streak, and share your little map. Friends play the same tiles in their browser.
  • Cozy acoustic music, seasons and festivals, and a dark mode.
  • Plays fully offline.

Fair and simple

Free to play, with a few ads that never interrupt the daily acre. Tiny Acres Plus is one optional, one-time purchase that removes them. No subscription, no in-game currency, no loot boxes.

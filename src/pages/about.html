About

Gather round

PrimeKamp makes mobile games for Google Play, with the App Store on the way, and runs the travel website TravelCharika. Games and journeys have the same home: the camp, where people gather to play and share stories.

The name

Prime means first and best. Kamp is the camp, spelled with a K so the name is ours alone. Together: the best place to gather.

The campfire

Our logo is a campfire drawn in pixels: the pixels are the games, the campfire is the camp where every trip starts, and one spark flies up.

How we make games

Calm, fair, and yours

  • The same puzzle for everyone

    Each game has one puzzle or deal a day, the same for every player, so you can compare notes with friends.

  • No lives, no timers

    Play at your own pace. There is no energy to refill and no clock to beat.

  • No pay-to-win

    Nothing for sale changes the day's puzzle. No loot boxes, no in-game currency, no subscriptions: each game has one optional, one-time purchase.

  • Ads that wait their turn

    Ads never interrupt the day's puzzle. An ad for extra help only plays if you choose to watch it.

  • Your progress stays with you

    No accounts and no sign-up. Your games are saved on your phone, not on our servers.

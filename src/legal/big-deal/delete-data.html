Delete your Big Deal data

Big Deal has no accounts and keeps your game data on your device, so you are always in control. We never receive it.

In the app

  1. Open Big Deal → Settings → Your data.
  2. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

This permanently erases your results, medals, streak, the deal in progress and your settings on that device. Your Remove all ads purchase remains linked to your Google account and can be restored from Settings → Big Deal Plus.

Without the app

Uninstalling Big Deal deletes its private storage, including all game data.

Android backup

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may also keep a copy of the game's save file in your own Google account backup (we cannot see it). To remove it, turn backup off or delete the backup in Android Settings → Google → Backup, or in your Google account's backup settings (for example the Backups list in Google Drive).

Advertising data

Advertising data held by Google AdMob is controlled through your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID / Reset advertising ID) and, in the EEA/UK/Switzerland, through "Ad privacy options" under Help and legal in the app's settings. If you own Remove all ads, the app shows no ads and does not start the ads SDK.

Anything else

Email brandemak@gmail.com from the address you want us to reply to. We hold no personal data about players ourselves, but we will help you with any request within 30 days.
